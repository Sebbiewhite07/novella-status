      A replay of recorded order books through the opportunity engine on validated pairs only. Figures cover episodes seen before the event. In-play episodes (after kick-off or the scheduled end) are counted but left out: one venue lagging the other, usually for one snapshot, is not fillable. Episodes on market pairs later found to be mismatched are left out the same way once the pair is withdrawn. Returns are net of fees at the size shown; size is the cost in dollars of the whole fillable quantity. The same resting orders often recur across episodes, so sizes and profits must not be added up. Limitless order-book sizes are overstated until a known fix lands, and AMM legs are unreliable for now. Predict.fun lists no end dates, so horizons use the other leg's scheduled end. Results fill in while the run is going.
