Net return and size are at each episode's best moment, after each venue's taker fees (Polymarket per-market rate, Predict.fun 2%, Limitless 3%→0.4% paid in tokens). The same resting orders recur across episodes, so sizes and profits must not be added up. Days to resolution use the scheduled end of whichever leg has one (Predict.fun publishes none); markets can resolve early or late. Settlement differs across venues (UMA, venue-run resolution), so no pair is risk-free. Categories are guessed from the question text.
